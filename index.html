Tripathi Laboratory — PCB Design

FEATURED · SUMMER 2026
GOAL

I'm the principal electrical engineer for the Tripathi Lab, a biomedical device R&D lab at Brown. My role is to design and build the custom PCBs the lab's research depends on, along with the high-voltage electrical setups used to power and control its biological experiments.

TOOLS / SKILLS

I run boards through the full design process: schematic capture and layout in Altium, hand assembly with soldering equipment for both SMD and through-hole components, and hot air guns for desoldering. I then test and iterate on the bench using multimeters, oscilloscopes, and waveform generators to confirm each board performs to spec.

RESULT

I've produced several working prototypes now in use in the lab, and expect to be named as a contributor on a few upcoming papers. This work is ongoing R&D toward new biotech tools, and I'm continuing it in the lab going forward.

Summer / Fall 2023

ANALOG DEVICES, INC.
GOAL

Design a printed circuit board (PCB) to demonstrate the ability of a new ADI product to interface with multiple types of sensors simultaneously — temperature, humidity, triaxial strain, triaxial acceleration, and a novel nanoengineered crack gauge.

TOOLS / SKILLS

To accomplish the goals, I needed to select available components and draw circuit diagrams, then learn component entry, schematic capture, layout, and routing using the industry-standard tool Altium Circuit Maker.

RESULT

By the end of summer 2023 I had finished selecting and entering components in Altium. I continued this work for ADI through the school year as part of an Independent Engineering Research Project for credit at my high school.

Summer 2022

ANALOG DEVICES, INC.
GOAL

Design a printed circuit board (PCB) to test the functionality of a new Internet of Things (IoT) product being developed by Analog Devices, Inc. (ADI). Specifically, ADI wanted to demonstrate the ability to communicate to different common digital protocols.

TOOLS / SKILLS

To accomplish the goals, I needed to read and interpret technical data sheets for sensor components, use PCB Artist for schematic capture, layout and routing, and program using a custom proprietary language created at ADI.

RESULT

The PCB I designed was fabricated and assembled at ADI. I uploaded the custom code to the EEPROM. The PCB used both SPI & I2C successfully. Finally, I created an instruction manual for customers to use this PCB and modify my custom code.

Summer 2021

METIS DESIGN CORPORATION
GOAL

Evaluate the range of GHz wireless inductive power & BLE data transmission through various common engineering materials as a function of thickness. My test matrix included various polymers, rubbers, composites and metals.

TOOLS / SKILLS

I designed the experiment to be safe and repeatable, and created a simple software procedure to determine if the power & data had been successfully transferred at a certain distance. The materials were purchased from McMaster and I stayed within my budget.

RESULT

At the end of the internship, I created a spreadsheet summarizing my results. I wrote up a summary paper of my findings, contributing to U.S. patent 11,499,621 issued 11/15/22 "Systems and Methods for In-Situ Monitoring of Gearbox."
